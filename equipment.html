OUR EQUIPMENT PHILOSOPHY

NO FILLER.
JUST PURPOSE.

BIOMECHANICS FIRST
Machines are evaluated for movement path, setup, resistance profile and how they actually feel under load.

VARIETY WITH A REASON
Different pieces should provide a different training stimulus — not duplicate the same movement just to fill square footage.

ROOM TO EVOLVE
Phase One establishes a complete training floor. Future additions will be driven by member use, feedback and the needs of the bodybuilding community.
